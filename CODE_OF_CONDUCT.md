# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and focus on the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not accepted.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@TING-HiuYu](https://github.com/TING-HiuYu). Reports are handled confidentially. The
maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may block
people who repeatedly do so.
